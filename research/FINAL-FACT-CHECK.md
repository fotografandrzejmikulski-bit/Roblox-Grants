This document is the final factual-control layer for the Aetheria grant package. It separates facts, project commitments, implementation evidence, and assumptions that require confirmation from Roblox or runtime testing.

## Red-line policy

No grant document may contain language implying:

- guaranteed funding;
- current participation in a Roblox program without confirmation;
- an official Roblox partnership that has not been signed;
- a completed gameplay benchmark that has not been executed;
- a shipped/public build that has not been published;
- team members or studios that have not been confirmed;
- access to experimental Roblox technology that Roblox has not granted.

## Final submission principle

The strongest application is not the one with the largest claims. It is the one where every material claim is either externally verified, directly demonstrated by the prototype, or explicitly framed as a forward-looking production commitment.
