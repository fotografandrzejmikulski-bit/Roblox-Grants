## B. Game Fund — statements that require current confirmation

The following must **not** be stated as current 2026 application facts without direct confirmation from Roblox:

- that the Game Fund is currently accepting applications;
- that the 2026 fund size is $25M, $35M, or any other amount;
- that the minimum grant is $500,000;
- that the maximum grant is $750,000;
- that funding is currently released in a fixed number of predefined tranches or percentages;
- that a specific Roblox employee or department is the current application point of contact;
- that the historical program criteria are unchanged in 2026.

The historical primary sources confirm the existence and structure of the program in 2021–2022, but they do not by themselves establish current 2026 terms. citeturn723596search0turn723596search7

## D. Claims from the working draft that require correction or primary-source verification

The original draft contains highly specific claims about:

- a "Text Generation API" with exact rate limits and context-window behavior;
- a "4D Generation API" replacing a legacy Mesh Generation API on a specific date;
- exact `GenerateModelAsync` behavior and automatic behavioral segmentation;
- "Roblox Guard 1.0";
- mandatory or automatic Restricted classification from a particular definition of extended AI interactions;
- exact requirements and fees for "Expedited Review";
- a 500 Highly Engaged Players threshold;
- a 100,000 Robux refundable deposit for expedited review;
- a 1,000 Robux publication fee/subscription rule;
- exact data-handling prohibitions attached to age-chat APIs;
- an exact 2026 funding ceiling;
- exact 2026 B2B Polish salary and Cieszyn office market assumptions.

These claims must be independently verified before inclusion in the submission. Where current primary documentation cannot be found, they should be removed, qualified as assumptions, or replaced with an architecture that does not depend on them.

## E. Evidence hierarchy

1. Current Roblox official documentation / current DevForum announcement.
2. Current Roblox corporate newsroom announcement.
3. Current official program/application page or direct written confirmation from Roblox.
4. Historical Roblox Game Fund announcements.
5. Reputable third-party research, only as contextual evidence.
6. Internal estimates and assumptions, explicitly labeled as such.

## F. Submission rule

Every material statement about eligibility, funding, API availability, platform policy, monetization share, age access, or required fees must have a current primary-source citation or be clearly labeled as a project assumption.
