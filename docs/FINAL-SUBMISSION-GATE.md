## 6. Evidence integrity

Every claim in the external package must fall into one of four categories:

1. **Verified current Roblox capability** — supported by current Roblox documentation.
2. **Historical Roblox program criterion** — explicitly labeled historical.
3. **Project design decision** — owned by the applicant; does not require Roblox to have announced it.
4. **Conditional / experimental integration** — clearly labeled dependent on availability and approval.

Never convert categories 2–4 into category 1 by wording alone.
