# Application / Partnership Letter — Aetheria: Echa Upadku

## Subject

Aetheria: Echa Upadku — Novel Multiplayer Experience / Roblox Partnership & Funding Inquiry

## Letter

Dear Roblox Creator Programs / Partnerships Team,

I am submitting **Aetheria: Echa Upadku**, a proposed Roblox-native cooperative survival-action RPG designed to demonstrate a new form of socially driven, high-fidelity multiplayer gameplay.

Aetheria is built around a simple proposition: the most valuable role for AI in a multiplayer game is not to replace the player, but to act as a bounded systems director that continuously adapts missions to the live state of a squad while preserving server authority, predictable gameplay rules, safety controls, and graceful failure behavior.

Players form squads of 4–6, leave a social hub, enter anomaly expeditions, coordinate reconnaissance and combat, make resource and extraction decisions, and return to improve their shared hub. The central Director system receives a deliberately limited gameplay snapshot and selects from a validated library of encounter and environmental interventions. AI is therefore used as a gameplay-orchestration layer rather than an unrestricted conversational interface.

The project has been deliberately structured around Roblox strengths: multiplayer interaction, cross-platform accessibility, mobile performance, avatar identity, social spaces, scalable content systems, and the platform's expanding AI-assisted creation capabilities.

Roblox's historical Game Fund demonstrated a clear interest in innovative multiplayer experiences with ambitious visuals, social mechanics, cross-platform/mobile requirements, avatar identity, and Avatar Shop integration. Roblox's 2026 Novel Games Incubator further confirms current strategic interest in novel genres, gameplay systems, visual approaches, multiplayer/social experiences, multi-device execution, and milestone-driven production. The first 2026 Incubator intake is closed; therefore this submission does not assume that a Game Fund or Incubator application is currently open or that any funding amount is guaranteed.

I am seeking guidance on the **current Roblox program, partnership, incubation, investment, or creator-support pathway** through which a project such as Aetheria should now be evaluated.

The attached package is organized to make technical and commercial validation straightforward. It includes a concise executive summary, game design, technical architecture, AI safety framework, financial model, milestone plan, risk register, go-to-market strategy, applicant profile, source register, and submission checklist.

The immediate proof point is a focused vertical slice demonstrating:

- a complete hub-to-expedition-to-extraction loop;
- a 4–6 player multiplayer session;
- a functioning bounded Director AI loop;
- deterministic fallback when AI services are unavailable;
- server-authoritative validation;
- representative mobile performance measurements;
- avatar identity presentation;
- a documented path toward launch and live operations.

The project is designed so that experimental or emerging Roblox technologies can accelerate the visual and systemic ambition without becoming a hard dependency for the core gameplay loop.

I would welcome the opportunity to provide the prototype, pitch deck, technical brief, financial model, and any additional information required for evaluation under the appropriate current Roblox program.
