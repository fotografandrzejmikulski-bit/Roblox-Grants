# Roblox Funding / Partnership Strategy — 2026

## Decision

The historical Roblox Game Fund remains relevant as evidence of Roblox's prior investment thesis, but it must not be presented as a confirmed, currently open 2026 grant intake. Roblox publicly announced the Game Fund in 2021, with funding starting at $500,000 per approved project, and published its project criteria and application materials at that time. Roblox later announced additional recipients in 2022 and stated a July 2022 application deadline in that announcement. citeturn996967search0turn996967search3

For 2026, the most directly relevant official creator-program evidence located is the **Roblox Incubator for Novel Games**. Roblox announced it on March 9, 2026 for ambitious creator teams with promising novel game ideas. The program emphasized novel genres, gameplay mechanics and visual styles; RPG, strategy, shooter and puzzle concepts; multiplayer/social design; multi-device support; visual ambition; team capability; and a realistic six-month roadmap plus a draft live-ops plan. The first 2026 cohort accepted applications through May 4, 2026 and is now closed. citeturn473887search0

## Strategic implication for Aetheria

Aetheria should be positioned as a **Roblox-native novel game / high-fidelity multiplayer project seeking Roblox partnership, investment, incubation or equivalent support**, rather than claiming that a specific 2026 Game Fund application is open.

The pitch should preserve the historical Game Fund alignment while using the 2026 Roblox Incubator language where appropriate:

- novel gameplay system;
- ambitious visual identity;
- strong multiplayer/social foundation;
- platform-avatar identity;
- cross-platform execution;
- mobile performance as a first-class constraint;
- realistic, milestone-driven production;
- launch and live-operations readiness.

## Submission route policy

Do not submit a document claiming an active Game Fund intake unless Roblox provides a current official application mechanism or written confirmation.

The preferred outreach package is therefore:

1. concise executive summary;
2. polished pitch deck;
3. six-month proof-of-concept roadmap;
4. technical architecture and AI safety brief;
5. prototype/video evidence;
6. realistic budget scenarios;
7. applicant/team profile;
8. direct request for the appropriate 2026 Roblox partnership/funding route.

## Required wording in external documents

Use:

> "The project is designed to fit Roblox's demonstrated investment thesis for novel, multiplayer, high-fidelity, socially driven experiences. We are seeking the appropriate current Roblox funding, incubation, partnership, or creator-support pathway available for this project."

Avoid:

> "Roblox Game Fund 2026 guarantees $750,000."

Avoid presenting historical Game Fund requirements as current contractual terms unless Roblox confirms them.
