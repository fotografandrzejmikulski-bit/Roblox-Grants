# Aetheria: Echa Upadku — Go-to-Market & Community Plan

## Launch philosophy

The launch strategy is staged. The project should not spend heavily on acquisition before the core loop demonstrates retention and technical stability.

## Stage 1 — Private prototype testing

Audience: invited testers.

Goals:

- validate the core loop;
- identify onboarding friction;
- test squad communication;
- measure expedition completion;
- benchmark device performance;
- test Director AI fallback behavior.

No commercial optimization is required at this stage.

## Stage 2 — Controlled external test

Audience: a broader but controlled test group.

Goals:

- establish early retention baselines;
- compare mission variants;
- measure social cohesion;
- test first progression systems;
- identify content that creates repeat sessions.

Paid acquisition is limited and only used to answer measurable product questions.

## Stage 3 — Soft launch / broader release

Goals:

- validate acquisition channels;
- establish stable KPI baselines;
- test monetization carefully;
- validate support and moderation processes;
- collect player feedback without over-expanding scope.

## Stage 4 — Scale

Scale paid acquisition only after the project demonstrates a defensible relationship between acquisition, activation, retention, session quality, and monetization.

## Community loop

The community plan should generate product value, not merely awareness:

```text
PLAYTEST
   ↓
PLAYER FEEDBACK
   ↓
ANALYSIS
   ↓
PRODUCT CHANGE
   ↓
NEW BUILD
   ↓
PLAYTEST
```

Community channels will focus on:

- expedition stories;
- development progress;
- behind-the-scenes technical work;
- controlled playtests;
- bug reports;
- feedback on mission modifiers;
- cosmetic and hub concepts.

## Creator / platform fit

Historical Roblox Game Fund communications emphasized innovative gameplay, ambitious visuals, social mechanics, cross-platform delivery, mobile optimization, platform-avatar identity, and Avatar Shop integration. ## Launch creative

The launch package should contain:

- a 30–45 second gameplay trailer;
- a 90 second systems trailer focused on the Director;
- short vertical clips demonstrating dynamic encounters;
- clean screenshots showing the social hub and expedition environments;
- a concise creator story;
- store-page copy optimized for clarity rather than hype.

## Acquisition guardrails

Paid acquisition must be paused when:

- activation falls materially below the validated baseline;
- D1 retention falls below the product threshold;
- crash or critical-error rates exceed the release threshold;
- mobile performance degrades;
- monetization produces a statistically meaningful retention penalty.
