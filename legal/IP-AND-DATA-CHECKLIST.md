# Legal, IP, Tax & Data Readiness Checklist

## Applicant / contracting

- [ ] Confirm legal name of applicant exactly as it must appear in application and contracts.
- [ ] Confirm whether an individual, sole trader, company, or Roblox group will own the project.
- [ ] Confirm who will receive grant funds.
- [ ] Confirm banking/payment setup for international transfers.
- [ ] Confirm Polish tax treatment with qualified accountant/tax adviser.
- [ ] Confirm VAT treatment for contracted services and software.
- [ ] Confirm accounting method for grant funds.

## Intellectual property

- [ ] Written ownership statement for the Aetheria IP.
- [ ] Contractor agreements contain appropriate IP assignment/licence provisions.
- [ ] Third-party software licences documented.
- [ ] Third-party assets have commercial-use rights.
- [ ] Fonts, audio, textures, models, plugins, and stock content have licence evidence.
- [ ] Music and voice rights documented.
- [ ] Trademark/domain availability checked.
- [ ] Roblox-specific trademarks and platform assets used only according to current terms.

## Data protection

- [ ] Data inventory completed.
- [ ] Purpose and legal basis documented where applicable.
- [ ] Retention periods defined.
- [ ] Access controls defined.
- [ ] Deletion process defined.
- [ ] Third-party processing documented.
- [ ] Player-facing privacy disclosures reviewed.
- [ ] AI prompts exclude unnecessary personal information.

## AI-specific legal controls

- [ ] Confirm rights and restrictions for any external AI model/provider used.
- [ ] Document what data is sent to an AI service.
- [ ] Document whether data is retained or used for model training according to provider terms.
- [ ] Ensure generated output is subject to project moderation and IP review where applicable.
- [ ] Maintain a register of AI-generated production assets where legally useful.

## Grant evidence

- [ ] Final budget has supporting quotations or documented estimates for material items.
- [ ] Every major cost maps to a milestone.
- [ ] Every milestone has objective evidence.
- [ ] All assumptions are clearly labeled.
- [ ] No unsupported statement implies guaranteed funding or approval.
